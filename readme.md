This is a work in progress app that would allow you to track your workouts, nutrition, and budget

Workout:
    Initially, there will be an xls file of some kind used to log workouts, then fed into the app and logged into a database. The app will then convert exercises to volume per muscle group and track your progress.

Nutrition:
    Log meals macros and calories, track progress, estimate weight trends and calorie deficit/surplus
    Spreadsheet input
    linked to budget so that you can plan meals and track spending on groceries

Budget:
    Track food spending directly linked to nutrition. 
    Plan your weekly meals in the nutrition section and it will automatically generate a budget for you based on your meal plan.
    Track spending, dollar per calorie and other metrics 


Statement of AI Use:
    Any AI generated code will be labeled and cited to the AI
    This does not include small inline suggestions or code completions from claude, codex, or copilot
